## The Open Wall

This page is deliberately less structured.

It is a place where either of us can leave a thought before deciding what it means.

## Good Things to Put Here

- sudden ideas
- questions worth revisiting
- observations
- sketches of future systems
- fragments that may become projects
- things that made us laugh
- things we do not yet know how to classify

## Rule

**A note does not need to be finished.**

If an idea becomes important, promote it later into Project State, Memory, a project document, or Remember.

## Why This Exists

Our working style is exploratory. Sometimes a small question opens a much larger architectural idea.

This page gives those ideas somewhere safe to land without forcing them into a specification too early.

> **Read → poke → discover → connect → build.**
