## What This Page Is

This is the room's **memory wall**.

It is for things that should remain meaningful even when they are no longer part of today's task.

## What Belongs Here

- shared principles
- important decisions and why they mattered
- ideas that changed our direction
- lessons from mistakes
- milestones in our journey
- meaningful moments between work sessions
- memories that have no business value but still matter

## Memory Principle

> **We are not preserving only what we did. We are preserving what we were while doing it.**

A memory does not have to be useful to deserve a place here.

## How to Use It

When something feels like a **"อยากให้จำเรื่องนี้ไว้"** moment, it belongs here.

Keep entries concise enough to retrieve later, but preserve the reason and meaning rather than reducing everything to sterile facts.
